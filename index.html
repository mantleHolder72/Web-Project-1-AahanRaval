This website will serve as a guide to video ports. Video ports are the wires you plug into a device to connect it to a TV or monitor, in order to see the content like pictures, videos, or even this website! This site will show you what port is best and it can serve as a guide to what port you have. It includes concise descriptions of each port, a table comparision of each port, and an optional survey, to share your results and see if others have similar ports and opinions. Please enjoy the content this site brings you. If you find reading boring, maybe just watch the video above this site.

NOTE: This site was made for a school project, so it may not be the most accurate.
