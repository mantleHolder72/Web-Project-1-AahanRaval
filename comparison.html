Video Ports

Comparison

This page will provide a comparison table for quick reference on the types of video ports, based off the information in Port Types. If you don't know about the Signal Type row, please reference the Signal Types page. If there are any mistakes or suggestions, please tell me in the Survey page.


[Video Port] [Maximum Resolution] [Availability] [Signal Type] [Use Case] [Recognizable Traits]
RCA (Radio Corporation of America) 480i@60Hz, 576i@60Hz (Component) Almost non-existent in newer devices, Common in older devices Analog Older device compatibility, CRT TV quality Red, yellow and white jacks (Component)
VGA (Video Graphics Array) 1080p@60Hz Uncommon in newer devices, Common in older devices Analog Older device compatibility, Vintage look 15-pin connector, blue casing
DVI (Digital Video Interface) 1920x1200@60Hz (single-link), 3840x2400@60Hz (dual-link) Rare in newer devices, Less common in older devices Analog and/or Digital (depends on cable type) Older device compatibility, Higher resolution than VGA Flat connector pins, white casing
HDMI (High-Definition Multimedia Interface) 720p@60Hz (Standard), 1080p@60Hz (High Speed), 4K@60Hz (Type 3) Common in newer devices, Almost non-existent in older devices Digital Device compatibility, Audio system compatibility Flat connector, "pressed-in" corners
DP (DisplayPort) 8K@60Hz (DisplayPort 2.1) Common in newer devices, Almost non-existent in older devices Digital Device comptibility, higher quality Flat connector, pins on top, diagonal corner



NOTE: This site was made for a school project, so it may not be the most accurate.
