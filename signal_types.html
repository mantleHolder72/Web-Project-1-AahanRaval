Video Ports

Signal Types

This section is a small explanation on the types of video signals. This section might have mistakes, so please tell me in the survey if there are any.

Analog

Analog video signals are sent in the form of electrical signals through the video cable. These video signals are converted to digital once they get to the monitor. This leads to a worse quality compared to digital due to signal conversion. Video standards that use analog are:

  • RCA (Radio Corporation of America)
  • VGA (Video Corporation of America)
  • DVI Digital Video Interface

Digital

Digital video signals are sent in the form of binary digits, easily recognised by the PC. The video signals require no conversion as the monitor already uses digital signals. This leads to having a better quality than analog video standards. Video standards that use digital are:

  • DVI Digital Video Interface
  • HDMI High-Definition Multimedia Interface
  • DP DisplayPort
